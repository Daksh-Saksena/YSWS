# The Tier and Streak System

Trek projects are funded based on both the time you spent on it, as well as the quality of the project. Bonuses are applied if you maintain streaks by actively journalling every day!

## Streaks

Streaks are applied as a multiplier to your usual payout when submitting. To increase a streak, journal at least once per day!

| Days | Streak Multiplier|
| --- | --- |
| 3+ | 1.02x |
| 7+ | 1.05x |
| 14+ | 1.1x |
| 30+ | 1.15x |
| 50+ | 1.2x |
| 80+ | 1.25x |
