# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Rules

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Who can participate?</summary>

Trek is open to builders aged 13 to 18. You must be a member of the Hack Club Slack.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Are hackatime-banned individuals allowed to participate?</summary>

Sorry, No!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I submit an existing project or one that I previously started?</summary>

If it's already finished, unfortunately not, sorry! Start a new project.
If you've just started or are in the middle of designing, you need to have journaled the entire process beforehand.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I double dip with Stardance, Stasis or any other Hack Club program?</summary>

No! Double-dipping is not allowed. If you have coding time for your project, you can journal it, and get coins to use in the shop!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">How likely is it that my project will be approved?</summary>

It's hard to say without seeing your project, but generally speaking, as long as you follow the [guidelines](/docs/requirements/submitting) on the submitting page for your project then you're more than likely to be approved!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I work on a team?</summary>

Yes! Using the collaborative Web journal feature!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I use a non-GitHub repo?</summary>

Yes!

</details>

## Funding & Reimbursements

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">How much funding can I get?</summary>

Technically unlimited! You get $5 an hour!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">How do I get my money?</summary>

Once your project is approved, you'll receive funding through a virtual card on [HCB](https://hcb.hackclub.com). Please keep and upload all of your receipts, since it is a requirement from the IRS!

> **We monitor your transactions.** Misuse of your funds will lead to a ban from Trek and potentially Hack Club as a whole. If you're not sure if something is an appropriate use of your funds, ask us before buying it!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I buy parts myself and get reimbursed later?</summary>

This depends on a case-by-case basis. Please ask in #trek-ysws before you make any purchases as some things you buy may not get approved or your whole project may not be eligible for trek.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I get reimbursed for 3D Printed parts?</summary>

Yep! We will partially reimburse you to the nearest 250g of filament.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">How will I get my 3D printed parts?</summary>

Through **Printing Legion!** It's an international network of Hack Clubbers 3D printing for each other! Check out [their website](https://printlegion.hackclub.com/).

</details>

## Building & Tools

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Do I need to know how to code?</summary>

Not necessarily — Trek is about hardware. If your project involves firmware, great. If it's purely mechanical, that's fine too. What matters is that you're designing and building something physical.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can we use tools like Ergogen?</summary>

You're free to use it to experiment with layouts and make design decisions, but the actual placement of the footprints and schematic has to be done manually by you.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I use auto-routing software?</summary>

No. You must do your routing manually.

</details>

## Time Tracking & Streaks

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Can I journal research?</summary>

Yes! **IMPORTANT:** Please log your research journals using [lapse](https://lapse.hackclub.com)!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Is hackatime or lapse url necessary?</summary>

Nope!, not necessary at all. But please justify your work in the journal substantially with images otherwise it will be subject to deflation.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">Do I need a lapse building my project?</summary>

Same story here. If not lapsed we might deflate time if the work to time ratio is inconsistent.

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">What is the purpose of streak system in trek?</summary>

The streak system increases your payout according to your streak, which increases for every day you journal!

</details>

<details>
<summary role="heading" aria-level="3" style="font-size:1.15em;font-weight:700;margin:0.5em 0;cursor:pointer;">What is the payout increase of streaks?</summary>

| Streak | Increase |
| --- | --- |
| 3+ days | 1.02x |
| 7+ days | 1.05x |
| 14+ days | 1.10x |
| 30+ days | 1.15x |
| 50+ days | 1.20x |
| 80+ days | 1.25x |

</details>
