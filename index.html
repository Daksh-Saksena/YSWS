What it is

Ship Something that moves. Earn Credits.

You Design something that actually moves - a drone, a rover, a boat, or something completely ridiculous - We Fund your build + extra credits!

"Don't know how to build hardware? Doesn't matter. Build whatever you want out of cardboard, motors, and hot glue. If it moves across the room, it counts, and you'll still earn Credits to spend in the shop. The big hardware kits are the bonus round, not the whole game."

How it works

  1. 1

  2. 2
    Ship it, track hours. Log your progress and prove your work.
  3. 3

Questions?

We've got answers.

Who can participate?

Any Hack Club member aged 13–18. No experience required.

What can I build?

Anything that moves! Planes, boats, submarines, rovers, walkers, RC cars, hovercraft — if it moves, it counts.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely. Trek is designed for builders at all levels. Your first project can be as simple as a rubber-band-powered boat.

Can I build with LEGO or cardboard?

Yes! Any material is valid. What matters is that it moves and that you built it.

Can I work in a team?

Yes, teams of up to 4 are welcome. Each member earns Credits for a completed project.

How do Credits work?

Credits are earned when you ship a completed, documented project. You then redeem them in the Trek store for parts, tools and kits.

When do rewards ship?

Rewards ship within a few weeks of your project being approved by the Trek team.
