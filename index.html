The Four Guilds!

Water Guild Master the seas. Build boats, submarines, hovercraft, and aquatic machines that conquer rivers, lakes, and oceans. If it floats, dives, or sails, you'll feel at home here.
Land Guild Conquer every terrain. From RC cars and rovers to walking robots and off-road machines, the Land Guild is for builders who turn rough ideas into unstoppable vehicles.
Frontier Guild Push engineering beyond the ordinary. Build experimental vehicles, space-inspired creations, futuristic concepts, or anything that doesn't fit the rules. Bold, ambitious, a little crazy.
Air Guild Take to the skies. Build drones, planes, gliders, rockets, and anything that flies. Whether you're chasing speed, precision, or altitude, the Air Guild is for those who dream above the clouds.
